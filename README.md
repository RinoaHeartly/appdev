## Overview
**The Walking Dead** is an *American post-apocalyptic horror television series* based on a comic book by **Robert Kirkman**.  
It tells the story of a group of survivors led by *Rick Grimes* as they navigate a world overrun by zombies and face moral challenges along the way.
> “We are the walking dead.” — Rick Grimes
---
### Main Characters (Ordered List)
1. **Rick Grimes** — A former sheriff’s deputy and leader of the survivors  
2. **Daryl Dixon** — A skilled hunter and tracker  
3. **Michonne** — A strong fighter who wields a katana  
4. **Carol Peletier** — A brave survivor with a caring heart  
5. **Negan** — A ruthless yet charismatic antagonist
